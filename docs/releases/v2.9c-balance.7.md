# v2.9c-balance.7 — Blade proficiencies and Battleluck

Released October 13, 2026, on top of balance.6. Two existing components change;
there are no new components.

- **Blade (1001, Blade Overhaul):** Blades may put two weapon slots
  (Specialization) into bastard swords, long swords, short swords, two-handed
  swords, katanas, scimitars/wakizashis/ninjatos, daggers, axes, spears and
  halberds. Automatic Two-Weapon Style grants stop at three slots, including
  the later level grants.
- **Battleluck (2008, New Bard Spells):** lasts five rounds and affects the
  caster and allies within 30 feet. Recasting refreshes it instead of stacking
  the bonuses, including the Gallant copy. Critical bonuses stay +1, rising to
  +2 at caster level 14, with protection from critical misses. The icon and
  expiration cue now end with the buff.

## Install

For a fresh installation, extract the archive into the game folder and run
`Setup-BardicWonders.exe`. Select **Blade Overhaul (1001)** and/or **New Bard
Spells (2008)** for these changes. The archive contains the full mod and keeps
every other released component unchanged.

An existing installation needs a normal WeiDU reinstall of the changed
component; copying the files alone does not update installed resources.
Existing characters are not retrained. Spell ceilings keep the released
optional 420 → 421 → 3010 policy. The separate Abettor tail stays at **1.1.0**.

## Checks and remaining limits

Release validation passes all **148** resource and installer tests, including
the Blade and Battleluck suites (added or reordered effects and abilities,
alternate proficiency tables, unrelated data preserved, repeated patches,
reinstall/uninstall and rollback of malformed inputs), and **48** WeiDU 249
syntax/version checks, including every shipped installer library. A combined
Chriz Easy BG test installation (game 2.7.3.0) installed both changed
components from the identical release candidate without errors. No in-game
playtest yet.

The existing Gallant component-order issue remains; the refresh covers the
Gallant copy when that copy is produced. The Darkbloom Spell Revisions and
Symphony party-invisibility limitations are unchanged. Making Heroism, Greater
Heroism and Potion of Heroism mutually exclusive is a separate, pending change
(see the [Heroism integration handoff](../heroism-family-integration.md)).
