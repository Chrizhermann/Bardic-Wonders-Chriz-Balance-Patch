# v2.9c-balance.6 — kit-specific bard spell progression

Optional component **3010** assigns different spell-slot progressions to installed
Bardic Wonders kits through the shared EEex provider in
[chriz-bg-rebalance v0.5.0](https://github.com/Chrizhermann/chriz-bg-rebalance/releases/tag/v0.5.0).
It updates their in-game descriptions to match and preserves existing bonus slots
and penalties.

## Progression and installation

| Kit | Base progression |
|---|---|
| Dancer | Original Baldur's Gate curve, through spell level 6 |
| Kapellmeister, Darkbloom | Icewind Dale curve, through spell level 8 |
| Abettor, Storm Drummer, Troubadour, Deathsinger, Strategist | Icewind Dale curve, through spell level 7 |

The Icewind Dale curves unlock spell level 7 at Bard level 21 and spell level 8
at Bard level 29. Dancer's slot penalty, Kapellmeister's two bonus slots, and
Darkbloom's bonus slot still apply. Special abilities retain their separate spell
access.

Install EEex and the desired kits and description-changing tweaks, then
chriz-bg-rebalance **420**, then Bardic Wonders **3010**. The provider's base Bard
policy **421** is optional; the collection uses **420 → 421 → 3010**. That optional
policy gives Bard/Jester the seventh-level curve and Blade/Skald the original
sixth-level curve. Without it, this adapter leaves native-kit registrations alone.

Darkbloom is optional and is skipped when absent. Component 3010 imposes no Spell
Revisions restriction; Darkbloom's separate copied-spell incompatibility under SR
remains tracked in issue #2. Mage/Bard multiclasses and Gallant are unaffected.
The engine hook is supplied once by the provider, rather than duplicated here.
The provider requires compatible Windows game instructions and EEex capabilities.

For an existing installation, append the new provider and adapter components at
the end of the stack. Do not reinstall older kit components just to enable this
feature. This release retains all balance.5 changes and the separate Abettor tail
at version **1.1.0**.
