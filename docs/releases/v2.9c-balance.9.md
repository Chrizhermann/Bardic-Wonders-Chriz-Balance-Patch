# v2.9c-balance.9 — Saving and resting during songs and passive effects

Recurring songs and passive effects could keep saving or resting unavailable
while their projectiles were active. This release gives those effects
save-compatible delivery. It is a bug fix for the frozen Chriz fork.

## Changes

- **Kit songs and passive effects.** Save-compatible profiles cover songs
  and their enhanced versions where applicable for Blade, Jester, Skald,
  Abettor, Dancer, Darkbloom, Storm Drummer, Troubadour and Kapellmeister.
  Skald's Combat Casting and Heroic Inspiration, Strategist's Combat
  Prowess, Battle Tactics and Counterattack Gambit, and Inspirations
  (2002) also use save-compatible pulses. Combat Casting refreshes the
  shared projectile in either install order with Artisan's Kitpack.
  Effects triggered by actual combat retain their usual save behavior.
- **Bard Song Mechanics Tweak (2004).** The installer rebuilds the finite
  wrappers for its stock and kit songs, including Bard, Blade and
  Deathsinger song paths and the BG2:EE stock Skald song when the
  Skald kit component is absent. Eligible songs supplied by
  other mods keep their delivery geometry, target filters and existing
  assignments through private projectile copies. The installer honors the
  legacy movement exemption, preserves unrelated effects and cues, and
  checks the song and available projectile IDs before registering its
  delivery. A song whose delivery cannot be installed stays intact while
  other songs can still be processed.
- **Bard class description and loader.** The finite-song loader is granted
  after a wrapper installs successfully. The class description reflects
  the active song system and selected equipment, casting, alignment and
  Inspiration options in either install order.
- **Icewind Dale: Enhanced Edition.** All six native modal songs keep
  their original controls and effects while gaining save-compatible
  projectiles. Native songs can be patched independently of finite
  wrappers. The stock Skald and Jester songs keep their IWD targeting and
  range, including when their kit components are installed separately.
- **Symphony of the Dark Children.** A usable Symphony keeps its HLA
  grant and finite-song links. If the optional invisibility cue is
  unavailable, the description follows the installed mechanics and omits
  the opening and finale Invisibility. See the
  [component 2004 compatibility notes](../abettor-component-2004-compatibility.md).

## Installation and verification

Extract the main ZIP into the game folder and run
`Setup-BardicWonders.exe`. Symphony uses Abettor (1004) and Bard Song
Mechanics Tweak (2004). The separate Abettor 1.2.0 tail remains the same
published archive and serves the existing standalone Abettor use case.

The release passed 211 automated tests and 54 installer syntax checks with
WeiDU 24900 across the main and tail TP2s and their TPA libraries. The
pre-release evidence includes 15 native WeiDU fixture checks across BG2:EE,
EET and IWD:EE. Coverage includes altered inputs, external song assignments,
the IWD modal path, projectile registration and the Symphony fallback.
Live in-game verification of saving, resting and Symphony's opening and
finale is pending.
